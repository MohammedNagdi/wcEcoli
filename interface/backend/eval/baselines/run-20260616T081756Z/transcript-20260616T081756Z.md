> The difference lies in their roles within the model:
> 
> **Dynamic state variables** are the concentrations of metabolites, proteins, RNAs, and other cellular components tracked over time (e.g., ATP levels, ribosome counts). They represent the system's "state" at any moment and are updated based on reaction fluxes and other processes.
> 
> **Reaction fluxes** are the rates at which reactions occur (units: mmol/gDCW/h), solved via *Flux Balance Analysis (FBA)*. >
